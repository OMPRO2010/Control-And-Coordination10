01 / NERVOUS SYSTEM

Detect. Process. Respond.

The nervous system coordinates the activities of the body and helps it respond to changes in the environment.

👁️

01

Receptor

⚡

02

Neuron

Carries electrical impulses through the nervous system.

💪

03

Effector

Muscles or glands produce the required response.

02 / COMMAND CENTRE

THE BRAIN

The brain is the main control centre of the nervous system.

FOREBRAIN

Thinking, memory, intelligence and sensory interpretation.

MIDBRAIN

Helps coordinate certain visual and auditory responses.

HINDBRAIN

Controls balance, posture and involuntary activities.
